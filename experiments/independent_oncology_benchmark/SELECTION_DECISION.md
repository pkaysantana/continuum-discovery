# Final Selection Decision
Timestamp: 2026-09-16T14:35:00Z

## Selected Candidate: EGFR L858R (4I22 Apo / 5D41 Holo)

**Reasoning:**
After screening 8 candidates against the frozen eligibility criteria, EGFR L858R emerges as the most robust candidate.
1. It is a highly relevant oncology therapeutic target (NSCLC).
2. It features a definitive, widely recognized cryptic allosteric pocket created by the outward displacement of the regulatory alpha-C helix.
3. The apo (4I22) and holo (5D41) structures have excellent construct parity (~330 residues), no massive truncations, and high resolution.
4. Comprehensive codebase and dataset `grep_search` audits confirmed `ELIGIBLE_NO_KNOWN_LACUNA_OVERLAP` and `NO_EXACT_ID_OVERLAP` for P2Rank.

CDK2 (1HCL / 3PY1) was also shortlisted but EGFR L858R provides a more modern, clinically validated cryptic allosteric pocket specific to mutant-driven oncology.

**EXPLICIT STATEMENT:**
Neither the Lacuna nor the P2Rank detector has been run on any candidate. The selection was made using ONLY the frozen eligibility and structural-quality criteria before downloading or processing final structures.
